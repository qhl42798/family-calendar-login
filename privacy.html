Privacy Policy — Family Smart Calendar

Last updated: 25 November 2026 · Contact: joeapps.support.au@gmail.com

Family Smart Calendar is a family calendar app. Your family calendar is stored on Supabase (Sydney, Australia). Only members of your family can view and edit it. The developer can technically access the data to operate the service, but will never use it for advertising or analytics.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address (or Google / Apple account) to identify you and which family you belong to.
  • Calendar content: event titles, times, locations, notes, reminders and repeat settings that you and your family create.
  • Family membership: family members, their roles (owner, admin, read-only) and invite codes (8-character codes, valid for 24 hours, single use).
  • Device push token: used only to notify you when a family member changes a calendar event. Push notifications do not contain event content.
  • Subscription status: your trial start date and, if you purchase, the product purchased and its expiry date, so the app can tell whether your family has access. Payment itself is handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No location tracking.
  • No accounts for children. Children are parent-managed profiles only (name and age); they do not log in and do not use the app directly.

Muse assistance

The app can be connected to Muse (your personal AI assistant) so Muse can add or edit calendar events for you. This only happens if you choose to connect it from Settings by generating an authorisation code. Muse can only access the family calendar you connected, and you can disconnect it at any time from Settings, which immediately revokes its access.

Sharing and retention

Your data is shared with no third party except Supabase (hosting, Sydney) and Apple (push notifications and in-app purchases). Your calendar data is kept while your family exists in the app. If you leave a family, you immediately lose access to that family's calendar. If the owner stops sharing or dissolves the family, the calendar is removed for all non-owner members. To request deletion of your account and data, email joeapps.support.au@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and the in-app version updated.

隐私政策 — Family Smart Calendar（家庭日历）

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 25 日 · 联系方式：joeapps.support.au@gmail.com

Family Smart Calendar 是一款家庭日历应用。你的家庭日历存在 Supabase（悉尼），只有家里人能查看和修改。开发者技术上能看到内容，不会用于广告或分析。

我们收集什么

  • 账号：登录时需要邮箱（或 Google / Apple 账号），用于识别你是谁、你属于哪个家庭。
  • 日历内容：你和家人创建的事项标题、时间、地点、备注、提醒和重复设置。
  • 家庭成员：家庭成员、角色（户主、管理员、只读）和邀请码（8 位，24 小时过期、只用一次）。
  • 设备推送令牌：只用于在家人改了事项时通知你有变化，推送里不带事项内容。
  • 订阅状态：试用开始日期，以及购买后的产品和到期日，用于判断你的家庭是否还有使用权限。付款由苹果处理，我们看不到你的付款信息。

我们不收集什么

  • 没有广告，没有分析或追踪工具。
  • 不追踪位置。
  • 孩子没有账号。孩子只是家长代管的档案（名字和年龄），不登录、不直接使用 App。

Muse 协助

App 可以连接 Muse（你的个人 AI 助手），让 Muse 帮你添加或修改日历事项。只有你在设置里主动生成授权码连接后才会这样。Muse 只能访问你连接的那个家庭日历，你随时可以在设置里断开连接，断开后授权立即失效。

共享与保留

你的数据不会分享给任何第三方，Supabase（托管，悉尼）和苹果（推送通知和应用内购买）除外。家庭存在期间，日历数据一直保留。你退出家庭后，立刻看不到这个家庭的日历；户主停止共享或解散家庭后，其他成员也一样看不到。如需删除账号和数据，请发邮件到 joeapps.support.au@gmail.com。

变更

本政策如有变更，会在这里更新新版本，App 内的版本也会同步更新。
